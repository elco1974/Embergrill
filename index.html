Recipe book

Pellet-grill favorites and a place to plan your next cook.

Smoky brisket · Low & slow

Prep 30 min · Cook 8–14+ hr · Grill 225°F

Ingredients: whole packer brisket, kosher salt, coarse black pepper, optional garlic powder.

  1. Trim hard fat, leaving about ¼ inch of fat cap.
  2. Season generously and let the rub adhere while the grill preheats.
  3. Smoke at 225°F until bark is set and the thickest part approaches 165°F.
  4. Wrap if desired; continue until probe-tender, often around 195–205°F.
  5. Rest wrapped for at least 1 hour before slicing against the grain.

Use tenderness and food-safety guidance—not temperature alone—to decide when it is done.

Pulled pork shoulder · Low & slow

Prep 15 min · Cook 8–12+ hr · Grill 225°F

Ingredients: pork shoulder, salt, pepper, paprika, brown sugar, garlic powder.

  1. Pat dry and coat with a light binder if desired.
  2. Apply seasoning and smoke at 225°F.
  3. Cook until deeply colored; wrap if desired to help manage the stall.
  4. Continue until probe-tender, commonly around 195–205°F.
  5. Rest, then shred and mix with the juices.
3-2-1 style pork ribs · Barbecue

Approx. 5–6 hr · Grill 225°F

Ingredients: pork ribs, dry rub, apple juice, barbecue sauce.

  1. Remove membrane and season both sides.
  2. Smoke unwrapped for about 3 hours.
  3. Wrap with a little apple juice for roughly 2 hours, checking tenderness.
  4. Unwrap, sauce lightly, and finish 30–60 minutes until tender.

Treat 3-2-1 as a guide, not a rule; rib thickness and grill conditions vary.

Weeknight smoked chicken · Hot & fast

Prep 15 min · Cook varies · Grill 375°F

Ingredients: chicken pieces, oil, salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder.

  1. Pat chicken dry and season evenly.
  2. Cook at about 375°F, turning as needed for even browning.
  3. Check the thickest part with a food thermometer; poultry must reach 165°F.
  4. Rest briefly before serving.
